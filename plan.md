## Later task: audience-led multilingual strategy
Use the owner's supplied top-10 country ranking as a market shortlist, then use CurioKids YouTube Studio analytics to select the top 3–4 language opportunities. Consider country views/unique viewers, watch time, retention, viewed-vs-swiped, subscribers, returning viewers, localization quality, voice availability and production cost. Spanish/Mexico is fifth in the supplied country ranking and should be compared with higher-ranked markets rather than retained by default. Do not change the publishing schedule or add languages until controlled pilots support the decision.
