## How it runs
- **Daily Short** (`.github/workflows/curio-kids-short.yml`, 08:40 UTC = 14:10 IST, PUBLISH_WINDOW default 16:00–19:00): rendered in the 2.5D **puppet theatre** (`pipeline/theatre.py`: curtains open and sit in front of the content, spotlight, footlights) in a teal and slate-blue playroom (`BG=kids`). Content uses big phone-readable text (Fredoka), pictures slide in from the wings, poems are karaoke-style, there are sparkle and confetti effects (`kids_fx.py`), and the end card is Subscribe / Like / Share. Uploads are Made for Kids, Education category, with the AI-content flag.
- **Weekly long video** (`.github/workflows/curio-kids-long.yml`, Saturday 04:30 UTC): `--long`, 16:9, from `long/LNN-*/episode.json`, with chapters and a 16:9 thumbnail.
- **Diagrams** (`pipeline/diagrams.py`): body (blood / nerves / air / food flows with real organ drawings), cycle (oval), journey, orbit. The camera stays on the stage during diagrams.
- **Real drawings:** `img:` from Bioicons (`assets/IMAGE_INDEX.txt`, 2,722 items with commercial-OK licences). Avoid labelled ones and the DBCLS anatomy and silhouette images (they render badly). Credits are added automatically.
- **Content:** 74 Shorts (ep002–ep075; the ep001 octopus was removed — it had been only an example), 49 with diagrams and 47 with real drawings. Long scripts L01–L08: heart and blood, eyes, water cycle, solar system, butterfly, brain and nerves, sleep, volcanoes.
