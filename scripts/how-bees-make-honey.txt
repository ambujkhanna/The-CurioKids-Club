LEO: Mia, how do bees make honey?
MIA: It starts with flowers! Bees sip a sweet juice called nectar.   | 🌸 Sweet nectar
LEO: Like drinking juice with a straw?
MIA: Exactly! Their tongue works like a tiny straw.   | 👅 A tongue straw!
LEO: Then what happens?
MIA: They carry the nectar home to the hive in a special honey stomach.   | 🏠 Honey stomach
LEO: A stomach just for honey? Ha ha! That's so silly!
MIA: At the hive, bees pass the nectar to each other and fan it with their wings until it gets thick.   | 🐝 Fan, fan, fan! ; until it's thick
LEO: So the wind from their wings dries it out?
MIA: Yes! Then they seal it in wax cells to keep it fresh.   | 🍯 Sealed in wax
LEO: Bees are busy little chefs!
